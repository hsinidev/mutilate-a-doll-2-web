Game Overview


Mutilate A Doll 2 Web is a browser game that challenges players to create the most creative, gruesome, and adorable dolls. Players must manipulate objects in a virtual world to build and design their own dolls, with the goal of achieving perfection.



How to Play



Controls:




    

Mouse Wheel:
 Zooms in and out of the game area

    

Right Click:
 Opens a menu for selecting objects

    

Left Mouse Button:
 Allows players to manipulate objects

    

Shift Key:
 Enables the use of the zoom feature in high detail mode






Objective:



The objective of the game is to create the most creative and unique dolls by manipulating various objects, such as fabric, threads, and other materials. Players must balance creativity with precision to achieve perfection.



Tips and Tricks for Success



Strategic Object Selection:




    
Choose objects that complement each other in terms of texture, color, and shape

    
Craft unique designs by combining different materials and objects

    
Use the zoom feature to inspect your creations and make adjustments as needed






Exploration and Experimentation:

Keep an eye out for hidden areas or secret items that can aid in your creative process
